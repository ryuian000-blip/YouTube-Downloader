Opening YouTube Downloader

Windows will warn you the first time. That's expected — here's why, and what to click.

Keep this folder together

Don't drag just the .exe somewhere else. The app needs the _internal folder sitting next to it. Move the whole folder wherever you like, then make a shortcut to the .exe if you want one on your Desktop (right-click → Show more options → Send to → Desktop).

The blue warning box

You'll see "Windows protected your PC". This appears for any app that hasn't been signed with a paid code-signing certificate — it isn't a virus warning, and it doesn't mean anything was detected.

  1. Click More info (small link in the dialog).
  2. Click Run anyway.

Once only. It opens normally forever after.

If your antivirus quarantines it instead, that's a false positive caused by the way Python apps are packaged. You may need to allow the folder in your antivirus settings.

Using it

Paste a YouTube link, press the green arrow, pick your options, hit Download.

Files go to your Downloads folder unless you click the folder path at the bottom and choose somewhere else. Your choice is remembered.

Optional: let Claude watch videos

This folder also contains ytdl-agent.exe, which connects the app to Claude Code so it can read a video's transcript and look at frames from it. Open a terminal in this folder and run: 

ytdl-agent.exe setup
