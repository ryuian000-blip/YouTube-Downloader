Opening YouTube Downloader for the first time

Your Mac will refuse to open it once, and that's normal. Here's the one-time fix.

This app isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate ($99/year), so unlike apps from the App Store or big companies, macOS can't verify who built it and blocks it the first time with a message like "Apple could not verify ‘YouTube Downloader’ is free of malware." The steps below clear that block. You only need to do this once.

1

Open Terminal

Press ⌘ Command + Space to open Spotlight search, type Terminal, then press Return. A plain window with white or black text will open — that's Terminal.

2

Copy this command

Click Copy, or triple-click the text below to select it all and press ⌘ Command + C. Then click inside the Terminal window and press ⌘ Command + V to paste it in.

xattr -cr ~/Downloads/"YouTube Downloader.app"

3

Press Return

Nothing will appear to happen — that means it worked. If you see red text mentioning "No such file", see the alternate method below; it means the app isn't in your Downloads folder.

4

Open the app normally

Close Terminal. Double-click YouTube Downloader.app — it'll open right up, and every time after this too.

If the app isn't in your Downloads folder
1

Open Terminal

Same as above: ⌘ Command + Space, type Terminal, press Return.

2

Type or paste this, but don't press Return yet

xattr -cr

Note the space at the end — it matters. If typing it yourself, it's exactly: xattr -cr then one space.

3

Drag the app into the Terminal window

Find YouTube Downloader.app in Finder (wherever you put it) and drag its icon onto the Terminal window. Its exact location will get typed in for you automatically.

4

Press Return, then open the app

Close Terminal and double-click the app — it'll open normally from now on.

You only need to do this once per Mac.
